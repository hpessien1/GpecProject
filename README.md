# GPEC Website — Company Profile and Website Brief

## Purpose

GPEC is an engineering company focused on electrical control and distribution panels, solar energy, and sales of electrical, solar, electronic, and industrial products. This website will give customers one clear place to understand GPEC's services, view completed and current work, find relevant products, and contact the company.

The site should work as both a company profile and a practical sales channel. Someone receiving its link should quickly learn who GPEC is, what GPEC can do, what GPEC has delivered, what GPEC is working on, and how to request a quote or speak with the team. Content should be understandable to both technical buyers and customers who need help choosing a solution.

> **Content rule:** Do not publish invented projects, client names, certifications, years of experience, product stock, prices, or performance claims. Replace placeholders below with verified GPEC information before launch.

## GPEC's Areas of Work

### 1. Electrical industrial control and distribution panels

GPEC designs, builds, constructs, and modifies panels used to control equipment and distribute electrical power in industrial and commercial settings. Work may cover new panels, replacement of outdated panels, expansion of existing systems, and modifications needed when equipment or processes change. A project should begin with the customer's load, operating requirements, available space, site conditions, and maintenance needs.

**Control panels** organize components that start, stop, monitor, and protect machines or processes. Depending on project requirements, a solution may include motor starters, contactors, relays, variable speed drives, programmable controllers, meters, switches, and operator controls. **Distribution panels** route power to circuits or equipment and may include breakers, isolators, busbars, metering, and protective devices. GPEC should describe its actual design, fabrication, wiring, installation, testing, and modification capabilities separately, so customers know which stages the team handles.

The website should show panel types GPEC has actually delivered, with photographs and project notes. Each example should explain customer need, GPEC's scope, key components, and outcome. Where appropriate, explain that final designs and installation must meet applicable project specifications and electrical safety requirements. List standards or certifications only when GPEC can verify them.

### 2. Solar energy

GPEC helps customers use solar power for homes, businesses, and industrial sites, according to its confirmed service scope. A solar project can start with energy-use assessment and system sizing, then move through equipment selection, system design, installation, commissioning, monitoring, and maintenance where GPEC offers those services.

Explain differences between grid-connected, off-grid, and hybrid systems in plain language. Show how panels, inverters, batteries, protection equipment, and monitoring work together. Customers should be able to ask for a site assessment or quote and understand which details to provide: location, current electricity usage, available roof or ground space, backup-power needs, and budget. Show only verified installations and measured results; avoid promising savings or output without a site-specific assessment.

### 3. Electrical sales

GPEC supplies electrical products used in building, maintenance, and industrial projects. This category should present the main equipment families GPEC actually sells, such as cables, breakers, switches, sockets, lighting, transformers, or distribution equipment, with clear distinctions between stocked items and items sourced on request.

Product pages should provide brand, model, specifications, application, availability, warranty information when supplied by the manufacturer, and a way to request a quote. Technical buyers should be able to identify the right item without calling first, while customers who are unsure should be able to ask GPEC for selection help.

### 4. Solar sales

GPEC sells major solar-system equipment, subject to verified stock and supplier relationships. This may include solar modules, inverters, battery systems, charge controllers, and packaged solutions. Explain what each product does and how buyers should compare options by capacity, compatibility, expected use, installation environment, and manufacturer documentation.

Organize listings by product type and use case. Provide data sheets when permitted, show whether installation is available from GPEC, and direct customers to request a system recommendation rather than selecting components solely by price. State stock and pricing only when GPEC can keep them current.

### 5. Solar accessory sales

Solar accessories are supporting components needed to connect, mount, protect, and monitor a solar installation. Examples may include mounting rails and brackets, connectors, solar-rated cables, combiner boxes, disconnects, surge protection, fuses, and monitoring devices. Publish only items GPEC actually supplies.

Product descriptions should say what the accessory is used for, relevant sizes or ratings, and which systems or components it fits. Compatibility matters: a buyer should be able to send panel, inverter, or battery details with an inquiry so GPEC can confirm the appropriate accessory.

### 6. Electrical accessory sales

Electrical accessories support safe installation, connection, protection, and maintenance of electrical systems. GPEC's actual range may include cable glands, lugs, terminals, conduits, enclosures, junction boxes, trunking, connectors, labeling materials, and mounting hardware.

The website should group accessories by function and give useful specifications such as material, dimensions, current or voltage rating where applicable, and intended environment. Buyers should be able to request bulk quantities, check compatible sizes, and ask about alternatives when a specified item is unavailable.

### 7. Electronics sales

GPEC sells electronic components or devices relevant to its customers, based on its confirmed catalog. Possible categories include sensors, power supplies, meters, control modules, communication devices, and testing equipment. Keep this category distinct from general electrical supplies by explaining each product's function and technical interfaces.

Listings should include model numbers, input and output requirements, operating range, connection details, and manufacturer information where available. Include a clear inquiry route for customers replacing a failed component or trying to match a device with an existing system.

### 8. Industrial sales

GPEC supplies equipment for industrial operation, maintenance, and upgrades. Depending on GPEC's actual inventory, this may include motors, drives, pumps, automation equipment, industrial switches, instrumentation, or power-distribution products. The site should explain target industries or applications only when GPEC has real experience serving them.

Industrial customers often buy against a specification or part number. Provide a quote form that accepts model numbers, quantities, technical requirements, delivery location, and drawings or photos. State whether GPEC can source alternatives and how compatibility will be checked before purchase.

### 9. Industrial accessory sales

Industrial accessories support installation, operation, protection, and maintenance of larger equipment and processes. GPEC's actual catalog may include control switches, push buttons, indicator lamps, terminal blocks, cable-management parts, sensor mounts, panel hardware, and replacement parts.

Help buyers search by equipment type, part number, rating, size, and manufacturer. Include a route for spare-part inquiries and make clear when GPEC needs a nameplate photo or technical drawing to identify a suitable replacement. Avoid presenting an accessory as universally compatible.

## Homepage Content Direction

**Suggested headline:** *Electrical engineering, solar solutions, and industrial supply from GPEC.*

**Suggested introduction:** *GPEC supports customers with electrical control and distribution panels, solar energy solutions, and a range of electrical, electronic, and industrial products. Explore our services and completed work, or tell us what your project needs.*

These lines are draft copy. Adjust them to GPEC's confirmed capabilities and preferred tone. Use real photographs of GPEC's work whenever possible. A strong homepage should show: what GPEC does, who it serves, evidence from completed projects, current activity, product categories, and a clear next step.

## Project Portfolio Requirements

Projects should carry the company's profile through evidence rather than broad claims. For each approved project, collect:

1. Project title, location, date or period, and customer name only with permission.
2. Customer problem and project requirements.
3. GPEC's exact role: design, build, modification, installation, supply, testing, or maintenance.
4. Equipment or materials used, without disclosing confidential details.
5. Before, during, and after images where available, with permission to publish.
6. Verified result and an optional customer testimonial with approval.

For work in progress, publish only information approved by the customer. Label status and last update date so visitors can distinguish active work from completed projects.

## Customer Communication and Quote Flow

Every service and product page should provide a direct contact action. Quote forms should ask only for useful information: name, company if applicable, contact details, service or product needed, quantity or project scope, location, preferred timing, and optional files. Send a confirmation to the customer and route requests to GPEC's sales or engineering contact. Show expected response time only if GPEC commits to it.

Offer phone and email links for immediate contact. Add WhatsApp only if GPEC has an official business number and can monitor it. Include consent wording and a privacy notice for submitted personal information. Protect uploads and limit access to inquiries.

## Design and Technical Requirements

- **Visual style:** Professional engineering look, readable typography, strong contrast, and a consistent palette selected from GPEC's actual brand colors. Use diagrams or product images when they clarify technical offerings.
- **Mobile use:** Pages, menus, forms, image galleries, and contact links must work well on phones as well as desktops.
- **Accessibility:** Semantic headings, keyboard-friendly navigation, descriptive link text, image alternatives, visible focus states, and form labels.
- **Performance:** Compressed images, responsive image sizes, limited animation, and fast loading on slower connections.
- **Search visibility:** Descriptive page titles, service-specific text, useful product metadata, business location details, and structured data where appropriate.
- **Trust:** Real GPEC photos, accurate contact details, dated project updates, verified credentials, and clear policies. Never use stock images to imply they show GPEC work.
- **Maintainability:** GPEC staff should be able to update projects, current work, products, and contact details without changing site code. Choose a content system that fits GPEC's editing capacity.
- **Security:** HTTPS, protected admin access, spam protection for forms, software updates, backups, and restricted handling of customer files.

## Content GPEC Must Provide Before Launch

- Official company name, logo, brand colors, short company history, and location.
- Phone number, email address, business address, hours, and social media links.
- Confirmed service scope for panel engineering, solar work, installation, and maintenance.
- Product categories, brands, specifications, data sheets, availability rules, and quote process.

- Approved details and images for current work.
- Verified licenses, certifications, warranties, or partnerships, if any.
- Privacy notice, contact-form recipients, and staff member responsible for website updates.

## Launch Checklist

1. Confirm every service description matches GPEC's real capabilities and every product listing matches its catalog.
2. Review project and customer information for accuracy and publication permission.
3. Test navigation, search or filters, contact links, quote forms, uploads, and confirmation messages on phone and desktop.
4. Check accessibility, page speed, image quality, spelling, metadata, and broken links.
5. Verify domain, HTTPS, backups, analytics preferences, and who will maintain content after launch.

**Definition of success:** A first-time visitor can understand GPEC's services, see credible examples of completed and current work, find relevant products, and contact GPEC about a specific need without searching elsewhere.
