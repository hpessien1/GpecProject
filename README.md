# GPEC Website — Company Profile and Website Brief

## Purpose

GPEC Engineering Company Limited ("GPEC Engineering") is an engineering service provider in Port Harcourt, Nigeria, focused on power, control, and electrical solutions: electrical and instrumentation (E&I) engineering, power and control panel building, installation, repairs and maintenance, solar systems, generators, security and smart home systems, and the sale of electrical, solar, electronic, and industrial products. This website will give customers one clear place to understand GPEC's services, view completed work, find and buy relevant products, and contact the company.

The site should work as both a company profile and a practical sales channel. Someone receiving its link should quickly learn who GPEC is, what GPEC can do, what GPEC has delivered, what GPEC is working on, how to buy products online, and how to request a quote or speak with the team. Content should be understandable to both technical buyers and customers who need help choosing a solution.

> **Content rule:** Do not publish invented projects, client names, certifications, years of experience, product stock, prices, or performance claims. Product prices come only from GPEC, through the admin panel. Replace placeholders below with verified GPEC information before launch.

## GPEC's Areas of Work

### 1. Electrical industrial control and distribution panels

GPEC designs, builds, constructs, and modifies panels used to control equipment and distribute electrical power in industrial and commercial settings. Work may cover new panels, replacement of outdated panels, expansion of existing systems, and modifications needed when equipment or processes change. A project should begin with the customer's load, operating requirements, available space, site conditions, and maintenance needs.

**Control panels** organize components that start, stop, monitor, and protect machines or processes. Depending on project requirements, a solution may include motor starters, contactors, relays, variable speed drives, programmable controllers, meters, switches, and operator controls. **Distribution panels** route power to circuits or equipment and may include breakers, isolators, busbars, metering, and protective devices. GPEC should describe its actual design, fabrication, wiring, installation, testing, and modification capabilities separately, so customers know which stages the team handles.

GPEC's profile confirms control panel design and building, industrial distribution panel and switchgear design and building (construction), and electrical and instrumentation (E&I) engineering, including instrumentation.

Where appropriate, explain that final designs and installation must meet applicable project specifications and electrical safety requirements. List standards or certifications only when GPEC can verify them.

## Online Ordering and Payment

Customers can buy catalogue products online. Prices are fixed and set by GPEC through a protected admin panel; price changes appear on the customer-facing site. At checkout, the customer chooses **pickup** (no delivery address and no delivery fee) or **delivery** within Nigeria (address required). Pickup is from GPEC's Woji address. The delivery fee depends on the customer's **delivery area**, chosen from a list of areas and fees that GPEC staff manage in the admin panel; products GPEC does not deliver can be marked pickup only.

Prices and payments are in **Nigerian naira (NGN)** (confirmed by GPEC, 2026-10-23). Payment uses **Paystack**, or a **bank transfer** to GPEC's Nigerian account (details entered by staff in the admin panel; staff confirm each transfer when the money arrives). GPEC wants customers to pay Paystack's transaction fee: it is added to the customer's total as a separate, visible line before payment. Paystack's published rate for local Nigerian payments is 1.5% + ₦100, with the ₦100 waived under ₦2,500 and the fee capped at ₦2,000 (Paystack's pricing pages, checked 2026-10-23); GPEC must confirm the rate on its own Paystack plan before checkout uses it. The customer is redirected to Paystack's hosted payment page and returned to the GPEC site afterwards. The website never collects or stores card details. An order counts as paid only after GPEC's server confirms the payment with Paystack. Detailed behaviour is in [`ECOMMERCE_REQUIREMENTS.md`](ECOMMERCE_REQUIREMENTS.md).

Custom work, such as panel design or a solar site assessment, is not bought online. It goes through the quote flow below.

## Design and Technical Requirements

- **Visual style:** Professional engineering look, readable typography, strong contrast, and a consistent palette selected from GPEC's actual brand colors. Use diagrams or product images when they clarify technical offerings.
- **Mobile use:** Pages, menus, forms, image galleries, and contact links must work well on phones as well as desktops.
- **Accessibility:** Semantic headings, keyboard-friendly navigation, descriptive link text, image alternatives, visible focus states, and form labels.
- **Performance:** Compressed images, responsive image sizes, limited animation, and fast loading on slower connections.
- **Search visibility:** Descriptive page titles, service-specific text, useful product metadata, business location details, and structured data where appropriate.
- **Trust:** Real GPEC photos, accurate contact details, dated project updates, verified credentials, and clear policies. Never use stock images to imply they show GPEC work.
- **Maintainability:** GPEC staff should be able to update projects, products, prices, and contact details without changing site code. Choose a content system that fits GPEC's editing capacity.
- **Security:** HTTPS, protected admin access with a record of who changed prices, spam protection for forms, software updates, backups, and restricted handling of customer files and orders.
- **Payments:** Paystack hosted checkout only. No card data on GPEC systems, amounts calculated by the server, and payment confirmed by the server before an order is treated as paid.
- **Hosting:** Hetzner Cloud server managed with Coolify, database on MongoDB Atlas (a tier with automated backups before launch), deployed by the project owner.
